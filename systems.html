Systems / 01—04

A large part of this work is delivered bespoke and privately for clients, so it cannot always be shown as a full public case study. This is the useful middle ground: a clear view of the problems, architecture and capabilities being built without exposing client data, credentials or engagement details.

Dealer OS

Dealer OS is a reusable operations layer for pre-owned luxury retail and other high-touch businesses. It combines Telegram-first intake, schema-adaptable Supabase data and n8n workflows for AI-assisted product capture, inventory tracking, listing syndication, customer notes and follow-up.

  • Inventory, catalogue and listing operations
  • AI-assisted product capture and enrichment
  • CRM views and customer intelligence
  • Reusable workflows across retail verticals

Bespoke retail operations layer

Dealer CRM Platform

A bespoke dealer CRM and operations platform for businesses that need catalogue, inventory, customer and financial workflows in one surface — without forcing the operating model into a generic CRM.

  • Catalogue ↔ inventory lifecycle
  • Bidirectional website publishing
  • Supplier, purchase and payment records
  • Expenses, recurring costs, invoice/PDF and reporting

Designed around a dealer's operating model

Talent Agency Management Software

A bespoke management platform for talent and creative agencies. When complete, it is designed to connect people, relationships, deals, campaigns, tasks, deliverables, meetings, revenue and secure client workspaces with role-aware permissions — plus a custom AI agent system that guides staff through the platform, surfaces next actions, assists with workflow intake and runs bounded automations under human control.

  • Talent, contacts and deal workflows
  • Campaign, deliverable and meeting coordination
  • Secure client workspaces and invitations
  • Custom AI agent for guidance, intake and bounded automation

Designed as a full agency operating system

Admin Panels

Secure control rooms for the data behind the product — designed to give teams direct control over the catalogue, workflows and operational details that keep the customer-facing experience moving.

  • Product, catalogue and data management
  • Image ordering, thumbnails and rich content
  • Roles, permissions, statuses and approvals
  • Reports, invoices, configuration and workflow utilities
