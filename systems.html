Dealer OS

Dealer OS is a reusable operations layer for pre-owned luxury retail and other high-touch businesses. It combines Telegram-first intake, schema-adaptable Supabase data and n8n workflows for AI-assisted product capture, inventory tracking, listing syndication, customer notes and follow-up.

  • Inventory, catalogue and listing operations
  • AI-assisted product capture and enrichment
  • CRM views and customer intelligence
  • Reusable workflows across retail verticals

Bespoke retail operations layer

Operations / 01—02

The storefront is only the visible layer. Behind it, SwiftLoop builds secure admin panels that let teams operate the catalogue, control the data and keep the customer-facing experience current without waiting on a developer for every change.

01Eyewear operations
Protected catalogue control

Eyewear Admin Panel

A secure catalogue and configurator control room for a multi-option eyewear business. Teams can manage product records, frame and lens combinations, imagery and availability while keeping the public configurator in sync.

  • Edit catalogue data and configurable product options
  • Order images and set the primary thumbnail
  • Manage frame variations, lens cuts, styles and colour/type choices
  • Apply protected availability and sold-state transitions
  • Keep invoice, brand, social and business-card tools in one workspace

Built for catalogue control at product depth

02Dealer operations
Protected inventory control

Watch Dealer Admin Panel

A secure operations workspace for a watch and accessories catalogue. It gives the team one place to maintain product data, reorder imagery, control inventory states and read the commercial picture behind the storefront.

  • Edit product captions, details, pricing and catalogue metadata
  • Drag-and-drop image ordering with thumbnail selection
  • Manage in-stock, reserved and sold states with protected actions
  • Track sold items through monthly sales and commission views
  • Connect catalogue changes to the wider inventory and payment workflow

Built for fast, auditable catalogue operations
